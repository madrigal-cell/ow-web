## 1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) are small files or records stored on your device when you visit a website. They make the site work, remember your preferences or measure how it is used.

## 2. Which cookies this site uses

This site is designed to work without cookies. Analytics cookies are only set if you expressly accept them in the notice shown on your first visit.

| Name | Type | Owner | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ow-consent` (local storage) | Technical | On White | Remember your cookie decision so we do not ask again | 12 months |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Analytics | Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) | Measure visits, page views and traffic sources in aggregate | Up to 2 years |

Vimeo videos load only when you press play, with "do not track" mode enabled. When played, Vimeo (Vimeo.com, Inc.) may set its own cookies under its policy.

## 3. How to accept, reject or withdraw consent

On your first visit you can accept or reject analytics cookies. If you reject them, the site works the same and no measurement cookie is set. You can change your decision at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, or by clearing the site's cookies and data in your browser settings. Instructions for the most common browsers are available in their help pages: Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.
