## Challenge

Xiaomi Spain wanted to launch the Xiaomi 17 Ultra camera without a single spec slide. The idea, "Comer con los ojos" (eat with your eyes): hand the phone to press and creators, take them out shooting with a real photographer, and end the day at Paco Morales' table at Noor, in Córdoba, in front of dishes inspired by what they had photographed.

Our job was to capture all of it for the brand's communications: photo and video of a day split between two very different settings, street and dining room, with guests who weren't there to pose.

## What we did

We followed the photo walk led by photographer Javier Corso, who coached guests on getting the most out of the phone as they went. We focused on what sells the product without saying so: how the phone is handled, what gets framed, people looking at the screen.

In the evening, at Noor, we covered Paco Morales' team in the kitchen and the dining room, and the dishes born from three inspiration photos: a baker, a door —the Puerta del Perdón— and a landscape chosen for its colours. Dinner, after-dinner and a flamenco tablao.

Afterwards, we selected and graded the photo coverage with a custom look for the brand, and went through it image by image with Xiaomi to separate what could be published from what couldn't: press guests, backgrounds, image rights. The video footage became the event film.

## Result

A tech brand talking about its camera without showing a spec sheet, and an image library ready to use from day one, already filtered for what can and can't be published. It's the kind of work brands and agencies value most: arriving at the event knowing what every photo is for.
