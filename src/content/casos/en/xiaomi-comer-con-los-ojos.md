## Challenge

Xiaomi Spain wanted to launch the Xiaomi 17 Ultra camera without a single spec slide. The idea, "Comer con los ojos" (eat with your eyes): bring photography and fine dining together at Noor, Paco Morales' restaurant in Córdoba. Put the phone in the hands of press, creators and photographers, and serve dishes born from images.

Our job was to capture all of it for the brand's communications: photo and video of an activation split between two very different settings, street and dining room, with guests who weren't there to pose.

## What we did

We followed the photo walk led by photographer Javier Corso, who coached guests on getting the most out of the phone as they went. We focused on what sells the product without saying so: how the phone is handled, what gets framed, people looking at the screen.

In the evening, at Noor, we covered Paco Morales' team in the kitchen and the dining room, and the dishes born from three inspiration photos: a baker, a door —the Puerta del Perdón— and a landscape chosen for its colours. Dinner, after-dinner and a flamenco tablao.

Afterwards, we selected and graded the photo coverage with a custom look for the brand, and went through it image by image with Xiaomi to separate what could be published from what couldn't: press guests, backgrounds, image rights.

And the film: a 1'07" 4K recap with photographer Javier Salas and Paco Morales. Salas explains how he translates a dish's construction, textures and colours into an image; Morales, how his cooking comes from Córdoba, its history, aromas and streets, and why he finds it innovative that diners can see and feel a dish before sitting at his table.

## Result

A tech brand talking about its camera without showing a spec sheet, with a three-Michelin-star chef making the case, and an image library ready to use from day one, already filtered for what can and can't be published. It's the kind of work brands and agencies value most: arriving at the event knowing what every photo is for.
